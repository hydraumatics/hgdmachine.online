Put your photos here (jpg, jpeg, png, webp or avif), using these exact names:

  hero.jpg                   home page banner (wide, subject on the right works best)
  division-machinery.jpg  division-hydraulics.jpg  division-parts.jpg
  division-consumables.jpg  division-power.jpg  division-safety.jpg
  how-we-work.jpg            "How we work" photo
  kelly-mafuta.jpg           owner portrait (square-ish, face in the upper half)
  logo.svg or logo.png       company logo (works on dark blue; PNG should be at least 288px tall)

The build converts them to AVIF/WebP in several sizes automatically. Missing files are simply skipped.
Photos already in public/images keep working as a fallback.
